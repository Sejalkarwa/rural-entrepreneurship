01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The resource is local.
The market doesn't have to be.

Rural and tribal communities already hold assets that can become businesses: traditional skills, crops, minor forest produce, craft knowledge and local identity. The missing links are often processing, packaging, finance, skills, logistics and market access.

01Raw produceLow value

Collectors and farmers often sell before processing or branding.

02Value additionHigher margin

Sorting, processing, design, quality and packaging move value upstream.

03Digital accessWider reach

Catalogues, open commerce, storytelling and logistics can connect village products to new buyers.

04Collective powerFairer terms

SHGs, producer groups and enterprises can aggregate supply, skills and bargaining power.

03 / THE ROOTS & RISE MODEL

Don't just sell the raw material.
Build the value chain.

The model connects producer capability with shared infrastructure and transparent markets. Each step creates a place for local entrepreneurship.

01

Identify

Map local crops, forest produce, crafts, skills and existing producer groups.

02

Upgrade

Add training, tools, quality standards, design, packaging and simple business systems.

03

Aggregate

Use SHGs, producer groups or enterprises to pool supply, equipment, transport and bargaining power.

04

Digitise

Create product catalogues, verified identities, stories, prices and fulfilment workflows.

05

Reach markets

Combine local fairs and institutions with e-commerce, open networks, retailers and B2B buyers.

06

Reinvest

Track margins and impact; reinvest in better tools, skills, youth jobs and sustainable sourcing.
